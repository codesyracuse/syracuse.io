### What this site is

A free, volunteer-maintained directory and calendar for the Syracuse, NY tech
community: user groups, events, venues, companies, and jobs. We do our best to
keep it accurate, but events move, listings go stale, and links rot. Confirm
details with the organizer before you show up.

a pull request. When you submit content you confirm you have the right to share
it, and you grant us permission to publish, edit, and remove it. We curate:
listings must be relevant to the local tech community, and we may decline or
remove anything at our discretion.
