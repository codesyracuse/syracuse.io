_Last updated: August 18, 2026_

Syracuse.io is run by CODESyracuse (the Coalition of Digital Engagements in
Syracuse), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. This policy covers syracuse.io and the
services we use to run it.

### What we collect

- **Nothing, for most visits.** Browsing syracuse.io does not require an
  account, and we do not set advertising or tracking cookies.
- **Your email address**, if you subscribe to our newsletter. That's it — we
  ask for an address and nothing else.
- **Payment details**, if you donate or sponsor an event. Payments are handled
  by [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy). We never see or store your card
  number. We do receive your name, email address, and the amount you gave so we
  can thank you and keep our books.
- **Standard server logs.** Our host records the usual request data (IP
  address, browser user agent, page requested) for security and reliability.

### How we use it

To send the newsletter you asked for, to acknowledge donations and issue
receipts, to meet our record-keeping obligations as a nonprofit, and to keep
the site online. Nothing else.

### What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share it with third
parties except the service providers listed below, and only so they can do the
job we hired them for. We do not run behavioral advertising.

### Service providers

- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy) — payment processing
- [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/privacy) — newsletter delivery
- [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) — hosting and CDN
- [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) — the source of this site is public and open

### Your choices

Every newsletter has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also email us at
[codes@syracuse.io](mailto:codes@syracuse.io) at any time to ask what we have
about you, correct it, or have it deleted. We'll honor the request unless we're
required to keep a donation record for tax purposes.

### Children

Syracuse.io is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.

### Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. The full history is
in [our public repository](https://github.com/codesyracuse/syracuse.io).

### Contact

Questions? Email [codes@syracuse.io](mailto:codes@syracuse.io).
